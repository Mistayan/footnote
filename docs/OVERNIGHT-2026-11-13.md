Q2 ([MISS-ANALYSIS.md](../bench/experiments/MISS-ANALYSIS.md), dev only). On SciFact and Alloprof, which have about one relevant document per query:

- **But many are close.** Alloprof: 130 of 216 misses sit at fused ranks 11 to 100; SciFact: 96 of 122. A second-stage reranker over the top 100 can reach them; nothing inside the first stage will.
- **Misses concentrate in long queries** (Alloprof 13+ words: 178 of 216).
