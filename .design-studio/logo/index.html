Footnote logo concepts

Three directions on the locked palette, each with the family: Footnote Library, Footnote Memory, Footnote Code. The moving dots behind are your company's data; the glass is Footnote, transparent, bringing it into focus. Glass refraction shows fully in Chromium browsers.

A · The lens numeral

wordmark + glass footnote

The wordmark in Plus Jakarta Sans; the footnote number becomes a small glass lens that refracts what passes behind it. In motion the lens drifts slightly over the data, as if reading it. Each product keeps the lens and changes the mark inside it.

B · The focus tile

glass app icon + wordmark

A rounded glass tile over a blurred field of data that comes sharp in its centre: your knowledge coming into focus. Works as the app icon, the favicon and the MCP server icon. The product is a lowercase f with its footnote mark.

C · The mark system

glass pills, footnote symbols

The brand is the set of footnote marks themselves, each in a glass pill. Calmest of the three, and the easiest to keep consistent in docs, the CLI and the installer output.
